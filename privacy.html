Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Fly Marketing Agency ("Fly Marketing," "we," "us") provides marketing and patient acquisition services to chiropractic practices. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website (www.flymarketing.agency) and the services we run on behalf of our clients.

1. Information we collect

When you visit this website. This site does not use contact forms, accounts or advertising cookies. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view, for security and performance.

When you contact us or book a call. If you email us, message us on social media, or book a strategy call through the scheduling form on our website, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, practice name, your answers to any booking questions and the contents of your message. Our scheduling form is provided by our CRM provider, and bookings may sync with our calendar.

When we provide services to a practice. When a chiropractic practice hires us, we run advertising, landing pages, text messaging, email, AI-assisted calling and appointment booking on that practice's behalf. In doing so we may process information that prospective patients submit, such as name, phone number, email address, appointment details and responses to questions. We process this information on behalf of, and according to the instructions of, the practice that collected it.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide our services
  • To run, measure and improve marketing campaigns for our clients
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders and follow-up messages on a practice's behalf
  • To maintain the security and performance of our website and systems
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Text messages and calls

People who submit their phone number through a client's landing page or form may receive text messages and calls, including automated and AI-assisted messages and calls, about their inquiry and appointment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. You can also ask to stop calls at any time.

Mobile information is not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers who help us deliver the messages.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • With the chiropractic practice a prospective patient contacted
  • With service providers that help us operate, such as hosting, CRM, messaging, calling, email and advertising platforms, who may use it only to provide their services
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our clients, the public or ourselves

5. Advertising platforms

We run ads for our clients on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. Those platforms may use cookies and similar technologies on client landing pages to measure ad performance, under their own privacy policies.

6. Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed to provide our services, meet our obligations to clients, and comply with the law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing us. If your information was collected on behalf of a chiropractic practice, we may direct your request to that practice. Depending on where you live, including California, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

8. Health information

Our marketing systems are designed to collect contact and scheduling information, not medical records. Please don't send detailed medical information through ads, forms or text messages. Your treatment information is handled by your chiropractic practice under its own privacy practices.

9. Children

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date above.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email marty@flymarketing.agency.
