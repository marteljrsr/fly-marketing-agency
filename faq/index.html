FAQ

Questions doctors ask us.

Do you work with insurance-based practices?

Yes, but we specialize in cash-pay and hybrid practices. The reason is simple: the economics work better. When a patient is paying out of pocket for a care plan, every lead that shows up and closes is worth more to you. Insurance-based practices have thinner margins per patient, so the ROI on paid acquisition gets squeezed.

If you're hybrid and your cash side is growing, we can work with that. If you're 100% insurance, we're probably not the right fit, and I'll tell you that on the call.

How much ad spend do I need?

Minimum $1,000 a month to start. That gives us enough data to read what the market is telling us within the first 7 to 10 days. We start there, watch the KPIs, and scale when the numbers justify it.

We don't let clients dump $3,000 in on day one before we've proven the creative and messaging are converting. That's how you burn money. Start at a grand, prove the model, then push spend up as patients come through the door.

What if I've been burned by agencies before?

Then you're exactly who I want to talk to. Most chiros I work with have been through 3, 4, 5 agencies. The reason you got burned is the same reason most chiros get burned: the agency was running instant lead forms that auto-fill patient info with zero intent. The patient doesn't even remember they filled it out.

We run a different model: a landing page that pre-qualifies before they ever book. Higher-intent leads, fewer no-shows. I'll show you exactly what your current agency is doing wrong on the call before you ever pay me a dollar.

How fast can you launch?

Five business days from the time you're onboarded and I have your assets. That's your landing page, automations, SMS sequences, AI bot integration and ad campaigns built and ready to go live.

I need your headshot, any testimonials you have, your price points and your calendar availability. Give me that and we're live inside a week.

Do I need to be on camera?

Not required, but it helps. Video ads with the doctor on camera consistently outperform image-only ads for cash-pay care, because patients want to see the person they're trusting with their body.

If you're comfortable on camera, we'll script it and you shoot it on your phone. If you're not, we can run image ads and UGC-style content and still get results; it just takes longer to build trust.

Is my patients' information secure and HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Everything we run for your practice is set up to be HIPAA compliant, from the landing page and CRM to the text messages and AI calls.

We only collect the contact and scheduling information needed to book the visit, never treatment records, and access is limited to your team and ours.

Will the AI sound robotic to my patients?

No. The text assistant writes like your front desk and sends as a blue-bubble iMessage. The voice agent sounds natural and conversational. Anything it can't handle gets passed to your team with full context, so no lead falls through the cracks.
